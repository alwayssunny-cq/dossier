# Brand

`docs/brand/CQ-BRAND-BOOK.md` is the canonical Closequarters Club brand book — identity, photography
direction, the three type tiers and their guardrails, voice, the banned-word list, and the four
colours. Read it before writing client-facing copy, picking imagery, or changing type and colour.

Two tests it asks you to apply, every time:

- Copy: could this have been written by Marriott Bonvoy? Then rewrite it.
- Imagery: does this feel like an advertisement? Then find another.
